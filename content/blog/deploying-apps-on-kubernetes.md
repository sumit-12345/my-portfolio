## Why Kubernetes?

Kubernetes gives you declarative workloads, scaling, and health checks. We'll walk through a sample deployment, service, ingress, and CI pipeline.

## Quickstart

1. Build container images
2. Push to registry
3. Apply manifests

## Best practices

- Use readiness and liveness probes
- Keep secrets in vault
- Autoscale by CPU or custom metrics
